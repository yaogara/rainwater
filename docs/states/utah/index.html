Rainfall & policy snapshot

Average annual rainfall
12.3 inches
Legality
Legal / Volume Capped & Registered under Utah Code Ann. § 73-3-1.5 (Senate Bill 32). Utah allows up to 2,500 gallons of rainwater storage with a free online registration with the Division of Water Rights. (Storage cap: 2,500 gallons maximum with free online state registration; up to 100 gallons without registration.)
Incentives
Salt Lake County and Utah State University Extension provide discounted rain barrel distribution.
