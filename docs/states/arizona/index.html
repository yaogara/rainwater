Tucson, Arizona · incentive

Tucson Water Rainwater Harvesting Rebate

Tucson Water offers a rainwater harvesting rebate of up to $1,999 per property under its July 2026 policy updates: up to $1,900 for qualifying catchment storage ($1.00/gallon for direct systems sized to capture at least 1 inch of roof rainfall; $0.50/gallon for undersized direct or indirect passive systems) plus an optional Treebate of up to $99 for planting 1–2 qualifying drought-tolerant trees ($50 for the first tree, $49 for the second).

Tucson Water single-family residential customers or small commercial customers (5/8-inch water meter) with active Tucson Water service prior to purchasing and installing the system. Harvested water must meet Tucson Water's Beneficial Use policy; optional Treebates apply only to new drought-tolerant trees on the TAMA Low Water Use Plant List watered by the rebated system. Application form reopened in July 2026 under updated One Water 2100 Plan rules. Rebate funding is limited; submitting a form does not guarantee funding, and Tucson Water may change or cancel the program without prior notice.
