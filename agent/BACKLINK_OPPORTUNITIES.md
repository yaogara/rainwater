# Structured Backlink Opportunities Framework

This registry organizes link development categories, target criteria, and initial candidate opportunities for `rainwaterdirectory.com`. 

> [!NOTE]
> No outreach emails are to be sent during setup. This framework is formatted for future programmatic scoring and relationship tracking.

---

## Opportunity Categories & Target Criteria

### 1. Listed Contractor Partners ("Featured On" Badges)
- **Target Profile**: Verified rainwater contractors listed on `rainwaterdirectory.com` who maintain an active website with a partners, credentials, or footer section.
- **Value Exchange**: Offer a lightweight, attractive SVG/PNG badge ("Featured Rainwater Harvesting Specialist on RainwaterDirectory.com") linking directly to their verified city listing.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - Dig Cooperative Inc. (`http://www.dig.coop/`) — Oakland, CA
  - Harvested Rain Solutions (`https://harvestedrainsolutions.com/`) — Dripping Springs, TX
  - Rainwater Collection Tanks (`https://rainwatercollectortanks.com/`) — San Marcos, TX
  - A-1 Tank LLC (`https://a1tank.com/`) — Paso Robles, CA
  - Merrill Precast & Supply (`http://merrillprecast.com/`) — Grass Valley, CA

---

### 2. Municipal Conservation & Water Utility Programs
- **Target Profile**: City water utilities running residential rebate programs that publish external resources or contractor lists for consumers.
- **Value Exchange**: Offer a vendor-neutral, non-commercial directory where residents can find qualified local contractors and state code guides.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - Austin Water Conservation (`austintexas.gov/department/water-conservation`) — Rebates up to $5,000 for rainwater systems.
  - Tucson Water (`tucsonaz.gov/water/rebates`) — Rainwater harvesting rebate program resource pages.
  - San Antonio Water System (SAWS) (`saws.org/conservation`) — Water conservation and rain catchment resource lists.
  - Santa Fe County Water Conservation (`santafecountynm.gov`) — Rainwater harvesting requirements & guides.

---

### 3. University Cooperative Extension Offices
- **Target Profile**: Land-grant university agricultural extensions publishing homeowner guides on rainwater collection, water quality, and cistern maintenance.
- **Value Exchange**: High-accuracy technical guides (filtration standards, sizing calculators) suitable for educational citations.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - Texas A&M AgriLife Extension (`agrilifeextension.tamu.edu`) — Extensive rainwater catchment library.
  - University of Arizona Cooperative Extension (`extension.arizona.edu`) — Desert harvesting and passive earthwork guides.
  - University of Florida IFAS Extension (`edis.ifas.ufl.edu`) — Rain barrel and cistern workshops.
  - Penn State Extension (`extension.psu.edu`) — Cistern and well management resources.

---

### 4. Rainwater Harvesting & Hydrology Industry Organizations
- **Target Profile**: Non-profit trade associations, watershed management councils, and regional conservation coalitions.
- **Value Exchange**: Industry directory listing, community advocacy, alignment on ARCSA standards.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - American Rainwater Catchment Systems Association (ARCSA) (`arcsa.org`)
  - International Rainwater Catchment Systems Association (IRCSA)
  - Watershed Management Group (`watershedmg.org`) — Tucson, AZ
  - Texas Rainwater Catchment Association

---

### 5. Green Building & Sustainable Architecture Portals
- **Target Profile**: Architecture, LEED certification, and net-zero building publications covering eco-friendly water systems.
- **Value Exchange**: Technical guides on integrating underground storage tanks and whole-house filtration in new construction.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - Green Building Advisor (`greenbuildingadvisor.com`)
  - Inhabitat (`inhabitat.com`)
  - Treehugger (`treehugger.com`)
  - USGBC Regional Chapters (US Green Building Council)

---

### 6. Homesteading, Permaculture & Off-Grid Living Publications
- **Target Profile**: Authoritative sites covering off-grid power, self-reliance, and independent domestic water systems.
- **Value Exchange**: In-depth pillar guides on IBC tote systems, off-grid water storage, and CDC-backed water purification.
- **Initial Candidates**:
  - Mother Earth News (`motherearthnews.com`)
  - Permaculture Research Institute (`permaculturenews.org`)
  - Off Grid World (`offgridworld.com`)
  - The Spruce (Gardening/Water Conservation sections)

---

### 7. Resource-Page Link Opportunities (.edu / .gov / .org)
- **Target Profile**: Curated "Useful Links" and "Recommended Resources" pages on state environmental department or NGO websites.
- **Initial Query Templates for Prospecting**:
  - `inurl:resources "rainwater harvesting" site:.gov`
  - `inurl:links "rainwater collection" site:.edu`
  - `"rainwater harvesting contractors" site:.org`

---

### 8. Environmental & Climate Journalists / Water Writers
- **Target Profile**: Reporters covering Western drought, aquifer depletion, stormwater flooding, and domestic water security.
- **Value Exchange**: Timely data quotes, localized rainfall trends, and policy overviews.
- **Initial Outlets**:
  - Inside Climate News
  - The Texas Tribune (Water & Environment beat)
  - High Country News (Western water rights)
  - Yale Environment 360

---

### 9. Broken Link Reclamation Opportunities
- **Methodology**: Crawl historic resource lists referencing defunct rainwater websites (e.g., dead rebate PDFs, shuttered regional non-profits) with HTTP 404/expired domains.
- **Action**: Offer updated equivalent pages from `rainwaterdirectory.com/blog/` or `rainwaterdirectory.com/states/`.

---

### 10. High-Yield Linkable Assets to Build
1. **US Rainwater Catchment Yield Calculator**:
   - URL: `/tools/rainwater-calculator`
   - Inputs: Zip code (auto-loads NOAA annual precipitation) + Roof Area (sq ft).
   - Output: Annual gallons captured, tank size recommendations, estimated water bill savings.
2. **State-by-State Rainwater Legality & Incentive Index**:
   - URL: `/states` (interactive map + comparison table).
   - Authoritative citation destination for writers researching state water law.
