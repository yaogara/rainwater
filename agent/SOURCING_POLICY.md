# Data Sourcing & Verification Policy

This document defines the strict sourcing hierarchy, citation rules, and verification standards required for all content, statistics, legal claims, and contractor listings on `rainwaterdirectory.com`.

---

## 1. Authoritative Hierarchy of Sources

When incorporating facts, regulations, or data points, agents and contributors must adhere to this hierarchy:

| Tier | Source Category | Examples | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Primary)** | Government & Hydrological Agencies | NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, USGS, EPA WaterSense, State Depts of Natural Resources | Climate normals, precipitation metrics, federal drinking water rules. |
| **Tier 1 (Primary)** | State & Municipal Governments | Texas Water Development Board, California DWR, City of Austin Code, Arizona Dept of Water Resources | State statutes, plumbing codes, local rebate amounts and permit guidelines. |
| **Tier 1 (Primary)** | Industry Standards Bodies | American Rainwater Catchment Systems Association (ARCSA), IAPMO (Uniform Plumbing Code), NSF International (NSF 53, 58, P231) | System design specifications, cross-connection standards, filtration certifications. |
| **Tier 2 (Secondary)** | University Extension Programs | Texas A&M AgriLife Extension, University of Arizona Extension, Penn State Extension | Agricultural practices, seasonal harvesting guidance, academic research. |
| **Tier 3 (Tertiary)** | Established Industry Publications | Green Building Advisor, Permaculture Magazine, Off-Grid Living publications | High-level overviews, case studies (must corroborate Tier 1 claims). |

---

## 2. Contractor Verification Standards

Before adding or updating contractor listings:

1. **Entity Legitimacy**:
   - Verify active registration in the state business registry (Secretary of State) or verified Google Business Profile.
   - Confirm that the company actively offers rainwater harvesting, cistern installation, or stormwater management services.

2. **Contact Accuracy**:
   - Phone numbers must be valid US telephone formats.
   - Addresses must correspond to verifiable commercial or service areas; no generic placeholder strings (e.g., "Texas, Texas") are permitted.

3. **No Fabricated Reviews or Badges**:
   - Review counts and star ratings must either link to verifiable public sources (e.g., Google Reviews) or be omitted.
   - Do not display "Certified" badges unless the contractor's credential (e.g., ARCSA Accredited Professional) has been directly validated against an official directory.
