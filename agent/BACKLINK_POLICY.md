# Sustainable Backlink Policy & Ethical Standards

This policy governs all off-page optimization and link development efforts for `rainwaterdirectory.com`.

---

## 1. Principles of Link Acquisition

All links earned or pursued must adhere strictly to Google Search Essentials and white-hat SEO principles. Our standard is: **Every link must provide genuine editorial value to human readers.**

### Permitted & Prioritized Link Strategies

1. **Linkable Assets (Data & Calculators)**:
   - Develop original, authoritative tools (e.g., catchment sizing calculator, rainfall normal datasets, state-by-state legal summaries) that industry publications, extension offices, and bloggers cite naturally.

2. **Contractor & Partner Citations**:
   - Invite listed rainwater contractors to display an official "Featured in Rainwater Directory" badge linking back to their verified profile or local city hub.

3. **Digital PR & Expert Commentary**:
   - Provide non-sponsored, expert insights on drought resilience, off-grid water systems, and stormwater codes to journalists, news outlets, and sustainability editors.

4. **Resource Page Inclusions**:
   - Suggest the directory to university extension programs, municipal water utilities, and watershed protection groups maintaining public lists of water conservation resources.

5. **Broken Link Reclamation**:
   - Identify dead or obsolete links on government, educational, or green-building sites pointing to defunct rainwater resources and offer updated, authoritative replacement guides.

---

## 2. Absolute Prohibitions

The following tactics are strictly forbidden under any circumstances:

- ❌ **Private Blog Networks (PBNs)** or manipulative webrings.
- ❌ **Purchased Backlinks** or paid sponsored posts without proper `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` attributes.
- ❌ **Automated Directory Submissions** or link blast services (e.g., Fiverr blasts, bulk submission bots).
- ❌ **Comment & Forum Spam**: No automated blog comment posting, Reddit link dropping, or Quora spam.
- ❌ **Reciprocal Link Schemes**: No mass "I link to you if you link to me" barters.
- ❌ **Fabricated Personas**: No outreach conducted using fake identities, spoofed emails, or deceptive pretexts.
- ❌ **Hidden or Deceptive Links**: No white-on-white text, zero-pixel anchors, or CSS-hidden links.
