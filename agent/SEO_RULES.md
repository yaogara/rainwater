# Core SEO Safety Rules

These rules are immutable operational constraints for all automated and assisted SEO workflows on `rainwaterdirectory.com`.

---

## 10 Mandatory Safety Principles

1. **Intent-Driven Page Creation**:
   - Never create pages solely because a keyword variation exists.
   - Every page must answer a distinct search intent that cannot be effectively satisfied by an existing URL.

2. **Substantive Local Value**:
   - Programmatic and location pages must contain meaningful, unique, localized value.
   - Pages consisting solely of generic templated boilerplate with swapped city names are prohibited. Every city page must show contractor source status, clearly scoped climate data, and relevant regional guidance.

3. **Zero Fabrication Policy**:
   - Never fabricate:
     - Contractors or business entities
     - Reviews or customer testimonials
     - Ratings or aggregate scores
     - Certifications or trade licenses
     - Physical addresses or phone numbers
     - Pricing, quotes, or hourly rates
     - Legal codes or municipal ordinances
     - Rebates, grants, or tax incentives
     - Scientific statistics or hydrological claims
     - Quotations or expert commentary
     - Water quality or lab testing results

4. **Primary Source Preference**:
   - All factual assertions, rainfall averages, legal statuses, and incentive programs must reference authoritative primary sources (e.g., NOAA, state water boards, municipal utility departments, ARCSA).

5. **Pruning & Consolidation Standard**:
   - Pages lacking sufficient unique value, receiving zero impressions over 90 days, or presenting duplicate intent must be:
     - **Improved** with verified local data,
     - **Consolidated** (301 redirect into a higher-authority regional hub), or
     - **Excluded from indexing** via `noindex` or canonical consolidation.

6. **Responsible AI Usage**:
   - AI is authorized to assist with research, schema generation, structure design, and editorial synthesis.
   - Scaled, unverified, or low-effort AI-generated content is strictly prohibited. Human editorial standards and verification apply to every deployed asset.

7. **Optimize Before Expanding**:
   - Prioritize optimizing existing URLs showing verified Google Search Console opportunity (positions 4–20 with substantial impressions) before generating new speculative content.

8. **Utility & Tool-Backed Content**:
   - Prioritize high-utility, interactive, and data-backed content (e.g., catchment sizing calculators, annual yield estimators, tank capacity matrices) that naturally earn editorial backlinks.

9. **Topical Authority Boundaries**:
   - Confine content strictly to the core topical cluster:
     - Rainwater harvesting systems (residential & commercial)
     - Local installers and certified contractors
     - Precipitation data and collection potential
     - Storage tanks, cisterns, and IBC totes
     - First-flush diverters and filtration stages
     - Legality, permits, and state/local plumbing codes
     - Rebate programs and water conservation incentives
     - System maintenance, winterization, and sanitation

10. **Zero Manipulation Link Standards**:
    - Never participate in:
      - Private Blog Networks (PBNs)
      - Automated blog comment or forum link drops
      - Mass directory spam or low-quality submission blasts
      - Paid link schemes or sponsored links without `rel="sponsored"`
      - Reciprocal link exchanges at scale
      - Fabricated outreach personas or automated fake outreach campaigns
      - Any manipulative practice violating Google Webmaster / Search Essentials guidelines
